02 / AI Native 创作AI Native creation

让想象，成为游戏From imagination to play

从一个“想玩这样的游戏”的念头出发，与 AI 一起搭世界、试玩法、画角色、写代码。人来决定什么值得做，再用真实的试玩，把一个想法慢慢打磨成值得再来一局的体验。It starts with a game we wish existed. With AI, we shape its world, explore the mechanics, create the art, and write the code. People choose the direction; real play turns the idea into something worth coming back to.

人定方向，AI 协作，每次试玩都更进一步。Human direction. AI collaboration. Better with every playtest.
  1. 01

    灵感Idea

    从一种想留下的心情，找到一个值得开玩的点子。Find a feeling we want to share, and an idea worth playing.

  2. 02

    世界World

    让角色、故事与规则，组成一个有自己气质的小世界。Give the characters, story, and rules a world of their own.

  3. 03

    原型Prototype

    先把核心玩法做出来，亲手试试它有没有趣。Build the smallest playable version and see if the fun is there.

  4. 04

    美术与代码Art & Code

    与 AI 一起，让画面、声音和交互贴合这款游戏的心情。Create with AI until the visuals, sound, and interactions feel like this game.

  5. 05

    试玩Playtest

    在手机和电脑上亲自试玩，发现不顺手的地方，反复打磨。Play on phones and desktops. Notice the friction, then refine it.

  6. 06

    上线与成长Launch & Grow

    把游戏交给玩家，让真实的反馈成为下一轮创作的起点。Let people play. Their feedback starts the next round of creation.

用 Codex 创作，让想象可以开玩。Crafted with Codex. Made to be played.
