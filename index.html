02 / 从创意到开玩From idea to play

AI Native 游戏制作流程AI Native Game Creation Flow

从一句创意开始，把世界观、角色、玩法、美术、代码和上线验证串成连续循环。AI 参与灵感扩展、原型打磨、内容生成和体验迭代，人类掌握方向，也亲自感受每一局。A single idea becomes a world, a cast, a playable prototype, and a game worth returning to. AI helps explore, create, and iterate; people set the direction and play every step of the way.

从“想玩什么”到“可以打开体验什么”，让每一次试玩推动下一轮创作。From “what would be fun?” to something you can actually play. Every playtest gives the next idea somewhere to begin.
