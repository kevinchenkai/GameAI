想追一阵热血、留在温柔里、入席江湖、让骰子带路、慢慢种花，或再来一次漂亮的三连？挑一种心情，直接开玩。 Chasing a rush, a quiet connection, jianghu banter, a lucky roll, a garden to tend, or one more satisfying clear? Pick the mood and step in.

Star Fighter 越战越上头 Chase the Rush

穿过弹幕、抢下强化，在越来越快的节奏里把反应推到极限。每次坠落，都只会让下一局来得更快。 Thread through incoming fire, grab the next power-up, and ride the pace as it keeps climbing. Every crash makes the next run harder to resist.

Soulmate 留在温柔里 Stay a Little Longer

聊几句心事，等一个表情，听她在夜里说晚安。这里没有催促，只有一段愿意陪你慢下来的日常。 Share a thought, wait for a smile, or hear a soft good night. Nothing rushes you here; it is simply a quiet moment made for two.

Wulin Tavern 入席江湖 Pull Up a Chair

推门就是一桌新故事：大侠拼桌、旧闻翻篇、妙语接连上菜。坐下来，看看今晚的江湖会聊到哪里。 Every table opens a new story: wandering heroes, old rumors, and replies sharp enough to steal the scene. Sit down and see where tonight leads.

Journey Ludo 让骰子带路 Let Luck Lead

和 AI 同伴踏上西游棋盘，一颗骰子就能让领先与垫底瞬间互换。下一格是奇遇，还是翻车？ Race AI companions across a Journey to the West board where one roll can rewrite the lead. The next tile may be a blessing or beautiful trouble.

Garden Match 慢慢种出好心情 Grow at Your Own Pace

旺财陪你轻松三消，花园也在一次次通关中悄悄长大。不赶时间，只收集一点今天的好心情。 Match at an easy pace with Wangcai beside you, then watch the garden grow with every clear. No hurry, just a little progress for today.

StackPop 七格之间，步步取舍 Make Every Slot Count

七格暂存槽把每一次点击都变成取舍。留对位置，等三枚同类“啪”地清空，压力也跟着一起消失。 Seven tray slots turn every tap into a choice. Save the right space, line up a triple, and feel the whole puzzle exhale with the pop.

AI Native 游戏制作流程 AI Native Game Creation Flow

从一句创意开始，把世界观、角色、玩法、美术、代码和上线验证串成连续循环。AI 不只是辅助写代码，而是参与灵感扩展、原型打磨、内容生成和体验迭代。 Start from a single idea, then connect worldbuilding, characters, mechanics, art, code, and launch checks into one creative loop. AI is not just a coding helper here; it shapes ideation, prototyping, content, and iteration.

适合快速把“想玩什么”变成“可以打开体验什么”，也适合持续扩展角色、剧情和玩法。 Built for turning “what would be fun?” into something playable, while leaving room to grow characters, stories, and mechanics over time.
